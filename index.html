Row & Repeat

Hands-free row counter for knitting and crochet.

Count your rows without putting down your needles

Row & Repeat keeps your row count while your hands stay busy. Tap +1 on the lock screen, double tap your Apple Watch, say "next", or press the Action Button. The app remembers that row 12 is a decrease row.

Download on the App Store (link coming with the release)

  • +1 and -1 buttons on the lock screen and in the Dynamic Island, no unlocking
  • Apple Watch app with a big counter, the Digital Crown and a complication
  • Voice mode: "next", "back", "undo", recognized on your iPhone
  • Repeat rules such as "every 6 rows, 8 times, starting at row 12"
  • PDF and image patterns with a ruler that moves down as you count
  • Projects with photos, yarn, needle or hook size, notes and time spent
  • Works offline. No account. Your projects stay on your devices.
