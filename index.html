The Pattern Book

No pattern yet? Pick one of 16 designs for scarves, blankets and cowls. Set the size and your yarn, change the colors or paint your own picture, and every row is written out for you. Then the counter tells you what to do on the row you're on. How big it comes out depends on your own swatch, so the app asks for one.

It knows your pattern

Tell it "every 6 rows, 8 times, starting at row 12" and it says "Decrease row! 3 of 8" when the row comes. Shaping lists the action rows ahead and how far away they are. Import a PDF or a photo, and a ruler keeps your row highlighted.

iPhone and iPad

iCloud keeps both on the same row. On an iPad, the pattern sits on the left and the counter on the right. Rows counted on two devices with no connection add up when they meet again: every count is its own record, so none can overwrite another.
