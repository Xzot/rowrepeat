Privacy

Row & Repeat collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking and no server of ours. On the App Store its label says Data Not Collected.

Effective October 30, 2026. Developer: Vlad Shchuka.

Where your things are

On your devices
Projects, counts, repeat rules, notes, photos, patterns, times and settings.
Between iPhone and Apple Watch
Counts and project details go directly between the two through Apple's WatchConnectivity.
In your own iCloud, if you turn on sync
The same projects, counts, patterns and photos, in the private CloudKit database of your Apple Account. It sits under your Apple Account, like anything else you keep in iCloud. We have no access to it. Turning sync off stops new data going up. To delete what's there: Settings > your name > iCloud > Manage Storage > Row & Repeat.
In a backup file, if you make one
It goes wherever you save it. The file isn't encrypted by the app.

Microphone and speech

Used only when you turn the microphone on in a project. "Next", "back" and "undo" are recognized on your device. Audio is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere. iOS asks the first time, and you can turn it off in Settings.

Photos and files

The app reads only the photo or file you pick for a project or a pattern. It doesn't browse your library.

Purchases

Row & Repeat Pro is sold by Apple. We never see your payment details: the app only asks Apple whether your Apple Account owns Pro.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13 and collects nothing from anyone.
