Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 30, 2026

Row & Repeat ("the app") is developed by Vlad Shchuka ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

What we collect

Nothing. We don't run any servers. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and it contains no third-party tools that collect data. In the App Store privacy label, Row & Repeat is listed as Data Not Collected.

Your projects and counters

Projects, counters, repeat rules, notes, photos you attach, session times and settings are stored on your iPhone. If you use the Apple Watch app, your counts are sent between your iPhone and your paired Apple Watch directly, using Apple's WatchConnectivity. The data does not pass through any server of ours. Deleting the app deletes its data.

Microphone and speech recognition

Voice mode uses the microphone and speech recognition, and only when you turn it on. Words like "next", "back" and "undo" are recognized on your iPhone. The audio is never stored, never sent anywhere and never reaches us. iOS asks for permission the first time, and you can turn it off at any time in iPhone Settings.

Photos and files

The app can only read the photos, PDFs and images you pick yourself, for example a project photo or a pattern. It does not browse your library. Imported patterns are stored on your device only.

Purchases

Row & Repeat Pro is sold by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit). We never see your payment details. The app only asks Apple whether your Apple Account owns Pro.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
