Counting

The Lock Screen counter doesn't appear

Open a project: that starts it. Check Notions > Counter on Lock Screen, and Settings > Row & Repeat > Live Activities on your iPhone. If it was closed, opening the project again brings it back.

Double tap doesn't work on my watch

It needs Apple Watch Series 9 or later, or Ultra 2 or later. On other watches, tap the screen or turn the Crown.

Voice doesn't react

The words are English in every language: "next", "back", "undo". The microphone stays on only while the project is on screen, and turns off after 30 minutes without a command. If your device has no speech model for English, it can't be used yet.

I counted a row by mistake

Undo takes back the last count, −1 takes one row off. To jump to a row, use the ... menu on the counter and Set Row.

iCloud and backups

My iPad doesn't show my rows

Both devices need the same Apple Account, iCloud turned on, and Notions > iCloud > Sync switched on. Sync is part of Pro, and Pro comes along with your Apple Account: tap Restore Purchases on the second device if it doesn't show up. Give it a minute after opening the app.

I counted on both devices with no connection

Nothing to do. Every count is its own record, so they add up when the devices reconnect.

Two devices changed the same project

Rows always merge. For the project's details (name, rules, counters) the newest save wins.

How do I make a backup?

Notions > Backup > Save a Backup writes one file with your projects, rows, patterns and photos. Keep it anywhere. Restoring only adds what's missing: it never removes anything.

Pro

I paid. Where's Pro on my new phone?

Notions > Row & Repeat Pro > Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account. It is one payment with no subscription. Family Sharing is on.
